# Cross-Functional-Change-order-and-Execution-Drift
Autonomous ECO Sentinel & Execution Synchronizer Closed-loop AI agent architecture eliminating Engineering Change Order (ECO) drift across PLM, ERP, MES, and QMS enterprise platforms.Executive SummaryIn complex discrete manufacturing (Aerospace & Defense, Industrial Machinery, High-Tech Electronics), approving an Engineering Change Order (ECO) in a Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) system represents technical design intent—not physical execution on the factory floor.   Due to structural mismatches between spatial Engineering Bills of Materials (EBOMs) and chronological Manufacturing Bills of Materials (MBOMs), engineering changes routinely fail to propagate to downstream operational systems. This disconnect creates Engineering Change Drift: technicians continue using superseded drawings, procurement orders obsolete components, and factories experience catastrophic rework and assembly line stoppages.   This platform acts as an Autonomous Enterprise Orchestrator, bridging PLM design releases directly with ERP inventory quarantines, MES standard operating procedures (SOPs), QMS quality inspection gates, and SCM supplier portals.   Quantified Industry ImpactCost Overruns & Delays: Design changes drive 56.5% of project cost overruns and 40% of schedule delays in complex manufacturing.   Rule of 10 Escalation: Catching an ECO non-conformance during field operation costs over 100x more than catching it during initial engineering design.   Factory Downtime: Unplanned assembly line stoppages at major aerospace OEMs cost upwards of $20,000 per hour.   Financial Reserves Required: Empirical data shows defense and aerospace acquisitions must hold up to 13.25% of development contract value in Management Reserve to cover un-budgeted ECO execution costs.   Key Capabilities & Closed-Loop WorkflowDynamic EBOM-to-MBOM Translation: Parses spatial geometry updates in CAD/PLM and automatically projects structural impact on shop-floor routings and work instructions.   Automated Impact Analysis: Evaluates proposed ECOs against live ERP inventory levels, active purchase orders, and supplier lead times prior to Change Control Board (CCB) sign-off.   Closed-Loop Execution Verification: Prevents an ECO from transitioning to "Closed" status in PLM until execution evidence (inventory quarantine, SOP update, supplier drawing receipt) is verified across target databases.   Automated Compliance Audit Packaging: Generates indexed, immutable audit packages compliant with ISO 9001:2015 Clause 7.5 and AS9100D Clause 8.1.2.   Regulatory Compliance CoverageISO 9001:2015 Clause 7.5 (Control of Documented Information): Guarantees current document availability at points of use while enforcing strict controls to prevent unintended use of obsolete drawings.   AS9100D Clause 8.1.2 (Configuration Management): Maintains complete, auditable configuration baselines across all product lifecycle phases.   AS9102 (First Article Inspection): Automatically triggers mandatory First Article Inspection (FAI) workflows in QMS whenever form, fit, or function changes occur.
